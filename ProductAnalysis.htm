TECHNOLOGY & USABILITY

Product analysis

My approach to understanding technology: start with the person using it, then consider the product’s real-world fit.

Technology support & guides

I’m interested in providing remote AI and tech support guidance and creating accessible technology product guides. Learning how a tool works is only part of the picture; helping people become comfortable using it matters, too.

01 / PURPOSE

Start with the need

Identify the task a person is trying to accomplish, and what would make the experience easier.

02 / USABILITY

Look beyond features

Consider setup, ease of use, accessibility, reliability, and how much guidance a new user may need.

03 / LONG-TERM FIT

Plan for support

Consider cost, updates, compatibility, privacy settings, and the availability of helpful documentation.

EMERGING TECHNOLOGY

Learning about AI

My interest includes AI that is not generative AI, as well as training people to use emerging tools. A useful guide should explain what a tool does, where it may help, and where users should be thoughtful about relying on it.

Building a learning mindset is important when adopting new technology.
