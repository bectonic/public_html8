Rebuilding my website

This fall I am attempting to rebuild my portfolio site from the ground up. Here is what is changing:
  • A cleaner layout with a new masthead and navigation bar
  • One shared stylesheet so every page looks consistent
  • Accessibility improvements like descriptive labels and readable color contrast
  • New pages for my product analysis and transcript

Exploring audio and AI

Outside of web design, I have been spending time with audio editing and digital media. I enjoy learning how small adjustments can make a recording sound clearer and more professional. I have also been experimenting with AI tools to understand where they are genuinely helpful and where a person still needs to do the thinking.
